Regatta Schedule & Notices of Race
for Canadian 505 Regattas

(and other regattas of particular interest to Canadian 505 sailors.)

This schedule lists Canadian regattas and others which may be of particular interest to Canadian 505 sailors. For a more complete Regatta Schedule and listing of Notices of Race, check out the North American Regatta Schedule on the North American 505 Home Page.

Click on hi-lighted contact name to see the e-mail address and phone numbers for the regatta contact. Click on hi-lighted event name to get the Notice of Race. Check the Regatta Reports & Results page after the regatta for a regatta report. For changes and updates to this schedule, please contact 505canada@robbert.ca

We strive to keep this schedule accurate and up to date, but cannot guarantee it is. Always contact a regatta organizer to confirm events rather than simply relying on this schedule.

Last updated April 23, 1999.
